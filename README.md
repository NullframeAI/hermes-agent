## Technical focus

A personal agent designed to grow with its operator over time: it accumulates context, adapts to how you work, and expands what it can do the longer it runs.

## Lab notes

- the lab's longitudinal autonomy experiment: what an agent becomes after months, not minutes
- growth curves feed `autonomy-probe` long-horizon research
